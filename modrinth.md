Fabric 1.21.1 port of Serene Seasons Better Winter. Deciduous leaves hide in late autumn and winter. Spruce and jungle leaves stay. Snow on a hidden leaf is not drawn, and small blocks hanging under that leaf are not drawn either. Hidden leaves and the snow on them have no collision and stop blocking light.

`config/serenebetterwinter-common.toml` chooses the sub-seasons. Embeddium and Distant Horizons LOD hiding are not in this Fabric port.

Requires Serene Seasons Fabric 1.21.1, Fabric API and Fabric Language Kotlin. License: GPL-3.0-only. Upstream: https://github.com/Maeiro/Serene-Seasons-Better-Winter
